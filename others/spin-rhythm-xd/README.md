# Spin Rhythm XD

Rhythm game by Super Spin Digital (PC, Switch, consoles, 2018-). The player turns a turntable-like wheel (mouse or controller) with two colored lanes. Notes (match, tap, beat, hold/slider, spin, scratch) arrive in perspective along a 3D "twisty" track. The in-game editor produces `.srtb` track bundles, shared on SpinShare (spinsha.re) as `.zip` backups with the bundle, album art and audio.

Category: **others**: notes sit on a wheel at positions -4..4 around the circumference of a curved 3D track and include spins/scratches. No fixed vertical lanes.
