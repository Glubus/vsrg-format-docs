# Samples: Malody chart package (`.mcz`)

This is a container-only format: the repository keeps NO archive. Real archives were downloaded on 2026-11-09, their chart files extracted (media discarded) and the archives deleted. The extracted charts and their provenance (source URL of the archive, date, sha256 of the original download) are listed in `malody/mc/samples/SOURCES.md`, in the section `Extracted from archives`: [../mc/samples/](../../mc/samples/); subfolders: `forge-demo_*/` subfolders (Malody Chart Forge exports) and the loose `malody-custom-charts_*.mc` files.
